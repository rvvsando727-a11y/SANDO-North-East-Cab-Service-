Day 1

Guwahati → Shillong

Day 7

Shillong → Guwahati

Breakfast, departure transfer and airport/railway station drop.

Culture & history

Know the place before you visit

The full website can carry a dedicated history article for each destination, with local culture, heritage, food and responsible-travel tips.

Meghalaya

Home to Khasi, Jaintia and Garo communities, Meghalaya is known for living-root bridges, sacred groves, distinctive matrilineal traditions and dramatic monsoon landscapes.

Assam & Kaziranga

Assam's history is closely connected with the Brahmaputra valley, Ahom heritage, tea culture and rich biodiversity. Kaziranga is globally known for the Indian one-horned rhinoceros.

Sikkim

Sikkim blends Himalayan Buddhist heritage, monasteries, Lepcha traditions and dramatic mountain landscapes, with Kanchenjunga as a defining presence.

Tawang / Arunachal Pradesh

Tawang is strongly associated with Monpa culture and Tibetan Buddhist heritage. The region's monasteries, high passes and mountain routes form a distinctive cultural landscape.
